Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how ToolNest (https://toolnest.com/) handles information when you use the website.

Information we collect

ToolNest does not require an account and does not ask for personal information to use its tools. Numbers, text and images you use in the calculators, converters and image tools are processed in your browser. ToolNest does not send them to its own server and does not keep them in a database.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you type are sent through a third-party form service (FormSubmit) to the site owner email inbox so that we can reply. We use this information only to answer your message. ToolNest does not store contact-form messages in a database. The form service processes the submission under its own privacy policy.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the time of each request. This is used to run and secure the service.

Cookies and local storage

ToolNest does not set its own cookies. The website uses your browser local storage to remember the names of up to four tools you used recently, so it can show a Recently used list on the homepage. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings. Third-party services described below may use cookies if they are added.

Analytics

ToolNest does not currently use an analytics service. If one is added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe it.

Advertising and Google AdSense

ToolNest does not currently show advertisements. We plan to apply for Google AdSense. If ads are enabled in the future, third-party vendors, including Google, will use cookies to serve ads based on a user previous visits to this website or other websites. Google use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ToolNest and other sites on the internet. This policy will be updated when ads go live.

Your advertising choices

If ads are shown, you can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Third-party services

The tools run in your browser. The currency converter currently uses fixed sample rates and makes no external requests. If a live exchange-rate service is added, your browser would contact that provider and this policy will be updated. Links to other websites lead to sites we do not control and their privacy practices are their own.

Image tools

Images you choose in Image Tools are processed on your device by your browser. They are not uploaded to ToolNest.

Your rights and requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete a message you sent through the contact form, email shoaib674191@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time. Because tool inputs are not stored by ToolNest, there is normally nothing else to delete. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under local data-protection law.

Children

ToolNest is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website changes, for example if analytics or advertising is added. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to shoaib674191@gmail.com.
