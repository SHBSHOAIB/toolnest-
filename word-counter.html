Word Counter

Count words, characters and reading time.

What is the Word Counter?

Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you type, and see the estimated reading time.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Read the counts, which update as you type.
  3. Use Reset to clear the text.

Formula

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces. Reading time = words ÷ 200 words per minute.

Example

A 1,000-word article takes about 5 minutes to read.

Important notes

Words are counted using spaces and line breaks, so languages that do not separate words with spaces may be counted differently. Sentences are detected from full stops, question marks and exclamation marks.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time estimated?

It assumes an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute.

Is my text stored?

No. It stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
