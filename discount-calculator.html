Discount Calculator

Find the sale price and how much you save.

What is the Discount Calculator?

Find the sale price and the amount you save from the original price and the discount percentage, with an optional tax rate.

How to use it

  1. Enter the original price and the discount percentage.
  2. Add a tax rate if you want the final price with tax.
  3. Press Calculate.

Formula

Savings = price × discount ÷ 100. Sale price = price − savings. With tax: final price = sale price × (1 + tax ÷ 100).

Example

A 2,500 item with 20% off: savings 500, sale price 2,000.

Important notes

The discount must be between 0 and 100 percent, and tax is applied after the discount. A store receipt may differ by a small amount because of its own rounding rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is tax added before or after the discount?

This tool adds tax to the discounted price. Some stores work differently, so check your receipt.

Can I combine two discounts?

Apply the first discount, then use the sale price as the new original price for the second.
