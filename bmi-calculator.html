BMI Calculator

Check your body mass index.

BMI is a general screening measure and is not a medical diagnosis.

What is the BMI Calculator?

Calculate body mass index from height and weight and see the general category. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

How to use it

  1. Choose centimeters or feet and inches for height.
  2. Enter your height and weight in kilograms.
  3. Press Calculate BMI.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight and 30 or more obesity.

Example

70 kg at 175 cm: 70 ÷ 1.75² = 22.9, which is in the normal range.

Important notes

BMI does not consider muscle mass, age, sex or body shape, so it can be misleading for some people. Height must be between 50 and 272 cm. It is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It does not measure body fat and can mislead for athletes, older adults and children. Ask a health professional for advice.

Can I enter feet and inches?

Yes. Choose Feet / Inches for height.
