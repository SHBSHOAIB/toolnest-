About ToolNest

What ToolNest is

ToolNest is a free website of calculators, converters and everyday tools that run directly in your browser. It is built to be quick to use, comfortable on a phone and clear about what each tool does.

Tools you can use

  • Calculators: basic calculator, percentage, discount and EMI
  • Converters: units and currencies
  • Construction: area and concrete volume
  • Everyday: BMI and electricity bill estimate
  • Text: word counter
  • Date and time: age calculator
  • Utilities: image resize, compress and convert

Who it is for

ToolNest is for anyone who needs a quick answer: students checking calculations, shoppers comparing discounts, homeowners and builders estimating areas and materials, writers counting words, and people preparing images for forms or websites.

What you can do here

Type your values, get the result straight away, then copy or share it. Each tool page explains the formula, gives an example and answers common questions so you can understand the result, not only see it.

Our commitment

We aim to keep the tools useful and accurate. Estimates are labeled as estimates, sample data is labeled as sample data, and the tools do not need an account. If you report a mistake, we will look into it and fix it. Please also read the Disclaimer.
