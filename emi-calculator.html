EMI Calculator

Estimate your monthly loan payments.

What is the EMI Calculator?

Estimate the monthly EMI, total payment and total interest on a loan from the amount, interest rate and tenure.

How to use it

  1. Enter the loan amount and the yearly interest rate.
  2. Enter the tenure and choose years or months.
  3. Press Calculate EMI.

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Example

A loan of 100,000 at 10% a year for 12 months has an EMI of about 8,792.

Important notes

The calculation assumes a fixed interest rate and equal monthly payments. Real loans can have variable rates, processing fees, insurance or prepayment rules, so confirm the final figures with your lender.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include fees or insurance?

No. It only uses the amount, rate and tenure. Banks may add processing fees and other charges.

Is interest charged on the reducing balance?

Yes. The standard EMI formula assumes interest on the outstanding balance.
