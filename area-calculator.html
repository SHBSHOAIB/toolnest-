Area Calculator

Area of rectangle, square, triangle and circle.

What is the Area Calculator?

Calculate the area of a rectangle, square, triangle or circle in meters or feet, shown in square meters and square feet.

How to use it

  1. Choose a shape and a unit.
  2. Enter the dimensions.
  3. Press Calculate Area.

Formula

Rectangle = length × width. Square = side². Triangle = ½ × base × height. Circle = π × radius².

Example

A circle with a radius of 3 m has an area of π × 3² = 28.27 square meters.

Important notes

Enter every dimension in the same unit. A triangle needs its base and its perpendicular height, not the slanted side. The circle uses the radius. Results are for flat, regular shapes only.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use feet?

Yes. Choose Foot and the result is shown in both square meters and square feet.

Is the circle input a radius or a diameter?

Radius. Divide a diameter by 2 first.
