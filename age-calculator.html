Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days.

What is the Age Calculator?

Enter a date of birth to see your exact age in years, months and days, plus the total days and hours you have lived.

How to use it

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Press Calculate Age.
  3. Read your age and totals. Use Reset to try another date.

Formula

Age is the difference between today and the birth date, borrowing days from the previous month when needed. Total days is the number of calendar days between the two dates.

Example

Born on 15 March 2000, on 10 October 2026 the age is 26 years, 6 months and 25 days.

Important notes

The tool uses the date and time zone of your device and rejects future dates. For legal or official proof of age, rely on your official documents.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count leap years?

Yes. Total days come from the real calendar dates, so leap days are included.

Is my date of birth saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
