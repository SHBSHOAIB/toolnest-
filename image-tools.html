What is the Image Tools?

Resize, compress or convert JPG, PNG and WebP images in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to a server.

How to use it

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Pick Resize, Compress or Convert and set the options.
  3. Press Apply, check the preview, then Download.

Formula

Resizing redraws the image on a canvas at the new width and height. Compression re-encodes the image at the chosen quality: lower quality gives a smaller file with less detail.

Example

A 4000 × 3000 photo resized to 1000 × 750 keeps its shape (aspect ratio) and is usually a much smaller file.

Important notes

Very large images use a lot of memory on phones, so sizes above 8000 px are rejected. JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas become white. Your original file is never changed.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Processing happens in your browser using Canvas.

Why did compression make the file bigger?

Images that are already well compressed can grow when re-encoded. Try a lower quality or another format.
