What is the Calculator?

A free online calculator for addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentages, built to be comfortable on a phone.

How to use it

  1. Tap the number and operator keys to build your calculation.
  2. Press = to see the result, or ⌫ to remove the last character.
  3. Use AC to clear everything and start again.

Formula

Multiplication and division are worked out before addition and subtraction. A number followed by % is divided by 100, and after + or − it means that percentage of the first number (200 + 10% = 220).

Example

50 + 20 × 2 = 90, because 20 × 2 is calculated first.

Important notes

Results are rounded to about ten decimal places so you do not see long floating-point tails such as 0.30000000000000004. Very large results may appear in scientific notation. Nothing you calculate is stored.

Frequently asked questions

Does it follow the order of operations?

Yes. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction.

How does the % key work?

On its own it divides by 100. After + or − it takes that percentage of the number before it.
