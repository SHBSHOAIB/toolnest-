Electricity Bill Calculator

Estimate your bill from units and rate.

This is an estimated calculation. Actual electricity bills may include taxes, surcharges, slab rates and other charges.

What is the Electricity Bill Calculator?

Estimate an electricity bill from units consumed, price per unit and fixed charges. This is a simple estimate, not an official bill.

How to use it

  1. Enter the units consumed.
  2. Enter the price per unit and any fixed charges.
  3. Press Estimate Bill.

Formula

Estimated total = units × price per unit + fixed charges.

Example

300 units at 20 per unit with 500 fixed charges: 300 × 20 + 500 = 6,500.

Important notes

This estimate leaves out slab rates, taxes, surcharges and meter charges. Use the average rate from your own bill as the price per unit. Units are usually kilowatt-hours (kWh).

Frequently asked questions

Why is my real bill different?

Real bills often include slab rates, taxes and surcharges that this simple tool does not model.

Which price per unit should I use?

Use the average rate on your bill or your provider tariff.
