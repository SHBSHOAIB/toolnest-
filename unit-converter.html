Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature and volume.

What is the Unit Converter?

Convert between common units of length, weight, temperature and volume instantly, with no sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Choose a category.
  2. Enter the value and pick the From and To units.
  3. Press Convert.

Formula

Length, weight and volume: value × (From factor ÷ To factor), using meter, kilogram and liter as bases. Temperature: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and K = °C + 273.15.

Example

5 miles = 5 × 1609.344 ÷ 1000 = 8.047 kilometers. 100°C = 212°F.

Important notes

Results show up to six decimal places. The gallon is the US liquid gallon, and Kelvin values below 0 are rejected. Always check the units before using a result in practical work.

Frequently asked questions

Is the gallon US or UK?

The tool uses the US liquid gallon (3.785 liters).

Can I convert length only?

Yes. Pick the Length category for meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, inches, feet, yards and miles.
