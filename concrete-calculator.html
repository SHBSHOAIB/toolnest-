Concrete Calculator

Estimate concrete volume for slabs and footings.

What is the Concrete Calculator?

Estimate the concrete volume for a slab or footing from length, width and thickness, with an allowance for waste.

How to use it

  1. Choose meters or feet.
  2. Enter length, width and thickness in that unit (for example 0.1 m for 10 cm).
  3. Set a waste allowance and press Calculate.

Formula

Volume = length × width × thickness. Order volume = volume × (1 + waste ÷ 100). 1 m³ = 1.308 yd³.

Example

A 5 m × 4 m slab that is 0.1 m thick is 2 m³. With 5% waste, plan for about 2.1 m³.

Important notes

The result is a volume estimate for a simple rectangular slab or footing. It does not account for reinforcement, sloping ground or formwork, and length, width and thickness must use the same unit.

Frequently asked questions

How much waste should I allow?

Many people add 5% to 10% for spillage and uneven ground. Ask your supplier for advice.

Does this give cement, sand and gravel amounts?

No. It estimates concrete volume only. Mix proportions depend on the concrete grade you need.
