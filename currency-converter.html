Currency Converter

Convert between 10 popular currencies.

Rates are for sample use only and are not live.

What is the Currency Converter?

Convert between ten popular currencies. The page currently uses sample rates, so results are for rough comparison only.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Choose the From and To currencies.
  3. Press Convert and read the amount and rate.

Formula

Converted amount = amount × (rate of target currency ÷ rate of source currency), with all rates measured against one base currency.

Example

With the sample rate of 1 USD = 278 PKR, 10 USD = 2,780 PKR.

Important notes

The rates in this tool are fixed sample values used for illustration. They are not updated, and they do not include bank fees or margins. Use an official source for real money transfers.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live exchange rates?

No. They are fixed sample rates. Check your bank or an official source before any transaction.

Why do banks give different rates?

Banks and exchange services add fees and use their own buy and sell rates.
